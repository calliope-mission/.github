09 · The figure, explored

Make the figure ours.

Drawn from scratch, but a figure with raised arms is one of the most common pictograms there is. On its own it is weak as a trademark. V1 is the chosen mark. V2 to V8 stay as explorations, for campaigns and future refinements.

V1 · Cheer chosen
The Calliope mark. Refinements in K16.
V2 · Lyre-bearer
Raised arms become the lyre. The voice sits inside it.
V3 · Spark head
The lyre’s diamond spark as the head. Keeps the old mark’s memory.
V4 · Held by the C
An open C around one person. Open on purpose: it lets people in.
V5 · Chorus
One voice raised, two beside it. The muse and her people.
V6 · Louder
Sound rising from the raised arms. The tagline, drawn.
V7 · Heavy cut
Thicker strokes for 16 to 24 px: favicons, badges.
Callope
V8 · The figure is the i
For the wordmark only, at large sizes.
