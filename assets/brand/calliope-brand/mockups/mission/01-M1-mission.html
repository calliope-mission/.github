Calliope · Mission
Calliope is a mission-driven organisation. One goal:

To free people from digital influence.

What is digital influence?

Software built to steer what you do, feel and believe, in its owner’s interest rather than yours. Most of it is invisible. More and more of it is automated, and AI is making it personal.

Louder than the Sirens.
01
Attention ranking
Feeds ordered to keep you scrolling, not to serve what you came for.
02
Retention alerts
Pushes, “we miss you” emails and streaks, timed to pull you back.
03
Recommendation systems
Autoplay and “up next” decide most of what gets watched.
04
Engagement design
Infinite scroll, likes and counters, built so a session never ends.
05
Tracking and ad auctions
Cookies build a profile of you that is auctioned in milliseconds.
06
Reward loops
Unpredictable rewards: the same schedule that keeps people at slot machines.
