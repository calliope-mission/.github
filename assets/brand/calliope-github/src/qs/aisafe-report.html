2.1 M1 Behavioural, psychological, emotional and sensitive profiling -- findings 3 | UNKNOWN 0 | decision requires_contextual_review | rules ran 1/1 [UNMEASURED]

Prohibited: Building, storing, retrieving or binding into a prompt a profile of the person that goes beyond the task: behavioural history, inferred personality or psychology, emotional state, or special-category traits; extracting such facts from the conversation for later use.
